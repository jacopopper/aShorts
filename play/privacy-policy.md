# aShorts privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

aShorts ("the app") is a digital-wellbeing app that blocks the Reels section of the Instagram app on Android. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

## Summary

aShorts does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal or sensitive data. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.

## Accessibility service

aShorts uses Android's AccessibilityService API. This is the core feature of the app, and you turn it on yourself, after an in-app explanation that asks for your consent.

- **What it reads:** only while the Instagram app is on screen, the identifiers and positions of on-screen elements (for example the Reels tab and the Reels viewer). Events from every other app are ignored.
- **What it does with it:** decides whether Reels is showing, covers the Reels tab, and closes Reels by pressing Back or tapping Instagram's Home tab.
- **What it does not do:** it does not read or record what you type, your messages, passwords, or the content of posts, and it does not track your activity.

Everything is processed in memory on your device, as it happens.

## Display over other apps

aShorts uses the "display over other apps" permission only to draw the cover over the Reels tab and the fallback screen over Reels.

## Data stored on your device

- **Settings:** your aShorts options (for example strict-mode wait time).
- **Detection log:** a short local log of what was detected (only the identifiers, labels and positions of the matched Instagram elements), so you can check that blocking works. It stays on your device and can be cleared in the app at any time. It leaves your device only if you share it yourself using the share button.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

## Third parties

aShorts contains no analytics, advertising or tracking libraries, and it shares no data with third parties.

## Children

aShorts is not directed at children under 13.
