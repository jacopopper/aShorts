aShorts blocks Instagram Reels. Everything else in Instagram works as usual.

TRULY FREE
Every feature, free. No trial, no subscription, no premium tier, no ads, no account.

LIGHTWEIGHT
About 1 MB. Does nothing while Instagram is closed.

PRIVATE
No internet permission, so nothing ever leaves your phone. No tracking, no analytics.

WHAT IT DOES
• Hides the Reels tab.
• Closes Reels instantly if one opens from a link.
• Strict mode: a countdown before you can turn blocking off.

WHY
Stop doomscrolling. Protect your focus and deep work. Build better digital habits and win back your time.

HOW IT WORKS
aShorts uses Android's Accessibility service. Only while Instagram is open, it reads the layout of the screen to find Reels, covers the tab and presses Back when Reels opens. It collects nothing. The app asks for your consent first, and you can turn it off anytime in Settings. It also needs "display over other apps" to draw the cover.
